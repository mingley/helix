What the evidence supports
Clinical labels and molecular effects are different endpoints. The prior heldout is already exposed; its model comparison is post hoc. No independent biological replication is established.


A measured regulatory endpoint
Reporter coefficients are source regression estimates in HEL 92.1.7 plasmid MPRA, not endogenous HBB levels or clinical probabilities. Cultures share one library; random constructs can contain multiple changes, and source coefficient p-values have acknowledged inflation limits.


Primary-human genome context
These are source-specific curated RefSeq annotations, not a complete inventory of functional sequence. Coverage uses merged interval unions. Uncovered sequence is not demonstrated nonfunctional.


All possible local SNVs
The complete 4,824-row map is in saturation.json. Splice flags cover canonical motifs and narrow boundary proximity; they cannot exclude cryptic splicing, regulatory effects or other isoforms.


Inputs, assumptions, software revision and hashes are in report.json. The CLI audit rechecks artifact integrity, the revision chain and scoped Lean proof. No remote scripts or network requests are used by this explorer.
